# Installing ThirstTrap from an APK

This is for people trying the app before it is on the Play Store. It covers
installing it, telling me which build you are on, and the one thing you must not
do to it.

## Installing

1. Download the `.apk` file I sent you.
2. Open it. Your phone will say it cannot install apps from this source, which
   is the normal warning for anything not from the Play Store and not a sign
   anything is wrong.
3. It offers a Settings link in that same dialog. Follow it, turn on the
   permission for whichever app you opened the file from, which is usually your
   file manager or your browser, and come back.
4. Play Protect may add a second warning saying the app was not checked. Tap
   "More details" and then "Install anyway". Play Protect flags anything it has
   not seen on the store before, so an unreleased app always gets this.

On a Xiaomi, Redmi or Poco phone there is an extra ten second scan before the
Install button becomes active, and it sometimes asks you to sign in to a Mi
account. That is MIUI, not the app.

Android 8 and newer is required.

## Telling me which build you have

Open Settings inside the app and look at the bottom line. It reads something
like:

    ThirstTrap 0.2.0 (226)

The number in brackets is the one that matters, because several builds can share
a name while I am changing things. Quote the whole line when you tell me
something is wrong, otherwise I cannot tell which version you were looking at.

## Updating

Open the new `.apk` the same way. It installs over the old one and keeps
everything, because every build I hand out is signed with the same key, and that
is what Android checks before allowing an update in place.

## The one thing not to do

**Do not uninstall the app.**

Your diary, the weights and your reminders can all be written to a backup file
from Settings, so those survive. Your photos cannot. They live inside the app's
own private storage rather than in your gallery, which is deliberate, because a
plant diary should not scatter dozens of near-identical pot photos through the
camera roll you share with other people. The cost of that choice is that
uninstalling takes every photo with it and no backup brings them back.

So if something goes wrong, tell me rather than reinstalling. Almost anything is
fixable with the app still installed and nothing is fixable afterwards.

Taking a backup from Settings now and again is still worth doing, for the same
reason it is worth doing anywhere.

## What happens when it reaches the Play Store

Nothing you need to do, as long as the store build is signed with the same key
as the APK you have. Then the store version arrives as an ordinary update, in
place, with your diary and your photos intact, and you can stop installing files
by hand.

If it ever turns out the store build carries a different signature, Android will
refuse to update and the only route across is to uninstall first, which is the
one thing above that loses the photos. I will tell you before that happens
rather than letting you find out from an error.

## What I am actually asking

Use it on your own plants for a week or two, and then tell me which parts you
reached for without thinking and which parts you had to work out. The second
list is the useful one. I would rather hear "I could not find where the weighing
lives" than a rating out of ten, because the first one tells me what to change.
